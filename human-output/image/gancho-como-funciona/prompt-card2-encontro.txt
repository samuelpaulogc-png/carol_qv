CAMERA: IMAX MK IV 65mm at ISO 250, elevated 40 degrees above the tabletop, oblique from the map's lower-left corner.
LENS: Hasselblad/Zeiss 80mm T2.2, stopped to T5.6, focus on the center of the map, far corner softening.
LIGHT: One hard 7.000K turquoise rim source behind the map grazing its engraved relief, soft cool top fill three stops under key. Void at 5 IRE, line highlights at 90 IRE, restrained halation, no haze.
SUBJECT: A tri-fold map carved from matte navy slate, half open, engraved with topographic contour lines. A raised turquoise light line shaped like a heartbeat trace crosses it through six identical small closed padlocks in brushed navy metal, ending at one mint-green point of light on the far edge.
FOREGROUND: Near fold edge in soft focus.
MIDGROUND: Line and padlocks sharp.
BACKGROUND: Seamless midnight navy void, no objects.
WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Slate absorbs light; contour grooves catch the rim as thin cyan lines.
MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Stone shows fine chisel texture and dust in the grooves.
POST BEHAVIOR: 65mm film grain structure, visible organic grain, palette ONLY deep navy, turquoise and one mint-green point, ZERO warm tones, orange or beige. No letters, numbers or logos.
COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Map on a diagonal across a wide frame, line rising left to right, negative space above.
