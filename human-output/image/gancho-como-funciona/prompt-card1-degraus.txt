CAMERA: ARRI Alexa 35 at ISO 800, floor-level at the base of the steps, lens tilted up 15 degrees, slightly oblique.
LENS: Canon K35 35mm T1.5, stopped to T2.8, focus on the second step, door softening.
LIGHT: Single motivated source: a narrow vertical slit of 6.500K cool white light through a door left ajar, turquoise cast on its edges, raking down across three glass steps. Zero fill. Slit at 95 IRE, step edges at 70 IRE, surroundings at 4 IRE, dry air, no haze or light beams.
SUBJECT: Three thick rectangular steps of smoked navy glass rising toward a tall plain dark door, open only a hand's width. Empty architectural still life, no people.
FOREGROUND: First step's front edge, soft, reflecting the slit.
MIDGROUND: Second and third steps, edges crisp with thin cyan lines.
BACKGROUND: Door and matte navy wall, all detail lost to black beyond the slit.
WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Glass absorbs midtones, only edges glow; matte lacquer door holds one vertical specular.
MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Faint scuffs on the glass treads.
POST BEHAVIOR: 35mm film grain structure, heavy organic grain in the blacks, palette ONLY deep navy, turquoise and cool white, ZERO warm tones, orange or beige. No letters, numbers or logos.
COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Steps climbing diagonally from lower-left, door slit on the right third as a vertical line of light, two thirds of the frame dark.
MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
