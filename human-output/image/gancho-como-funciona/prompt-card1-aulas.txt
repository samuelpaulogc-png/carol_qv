CAMERA: IMAX MK IV 65mm at ISO 250, low at tabletop height, oblique 30 degrees to the object, lens axis tilted slightly upward.
LENS: Zeiss Makro-Planar 65mm T2.2, stopped to T4, focus on the front slab edge, rear slabs softening.

MIDGROUND: Slabs crisp on their front edges.
BACKGROUND: Seamless midnight navy void, faint cool falloff, no objects.
WARDROBE TONAL BEHAVIOR: Glass absorbs midtones; brushed turquoise metal trim on the edges catches the rim.
MAKEUP SURFACE PHYSICS: Glass shows micro-scratches and faint internal bubbles.
